# home-page-others

A collection of 4 distinct, modern, responsive school and college homepage designs built with PHP, Vanilla CSS, and JavaScript.

## 🚀 Homepages Included

1. **Home 1 (Classic Institutional)** - `index.php`
   - Complete school & college portal with principal & chairman messages, notice board, events, faculty roster, photo/video archive, online admission modal, and student portal.

2. **Home 2 (Smart Campus & Bento Grid)** - `home2.php`
   - Modern Bento-grid layout, live interactive tuition fee calculator, digital academic tracks, club activities, and technology-enabled smart campus showcase.

3. **Home 3 (Green Valley Model School & College)** - `home3.php`
   - Authentic Bangladeshi school style with bilingual typography (Bangla & English), national curriculum focus, daily prayer/routine strip, notice board, and board exam statistics.

4. **Home 4 (Rivermount School - Minimal Black & White)** - `home4.php`
   - High-contrast minimal design with "Learn Grow Lead" typography, 6 key institutional features, academic stage cards (Primary, Secondary, Higher Secondary), facility tiles, split notices & events, and parent testimonials.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack
- **Backend:** PHP 8.x
- **Frontend:** HTML5, Vanilla CSS3 (Custom Design System), JavaScript (ES6)
- **Icons & Fonts:** Font Awesome 6, Google Fonts (Plus Jakarta Sans, Outfit, Playfair Display, Caveat, Hind Siliguri)

## 💻 Running Locally
```bash
php -S 127.0.0.1:8080
```
Open `http://127.0.0.1:8080/index.php` or `http://127.0.0.1:8080/home4.php` in your browser.
